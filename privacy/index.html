Privacy Policy

Seamen English Rules Pool

Seamen English Rules Pool does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.

Game progress, statistics, settings and other save information are stored locally on your device. The game provides save export and import features which allow you to create and restore your own save files. These files remain under your control and are not transmitted to Seamen Games.

The game does not contain advertising or third-party analytics and does not use tracking technologies for advertising or profiling.

Seamen English Rules Pool does not require a user account.

The game may contain links which open external websites or services. Information subsequently provided to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.
