Privacy Policy

Seamen English Rules Pool

Seamen English Rules Pool does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.

Game progress, statistics, settings and other save information are stored locally on your device. The game provides save export and import features which allow you to create and restore your own save files. These files remain under your control and are not transmitted to Seamen Games.

The game does not contain advertising or third-party analytics and does not use tracking technologies for advertising or profiling.

Seamen English Rules Pool does not require a user account.

On Android, optional paid content may be purchased and restored through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments and checks purchase ownership; Seamen Games does not receive your payment card details. Google may process purchase and device-related information to operate these services under Google's Privacy Policy. Purchase records held by Google are separate from locally stored game saves.

Android and Google Play may provide platform services and diagnostics independently of the game, subject to Google's settings and policies. If you choose to export a save file, you control where you store or share it; third-party destinations have their own privacy practices.

The game may contain links which open external websites or services. Information subsequently provided to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Seamen Banker

Seamen Banker is a fictional, single-player bank-management game. It does not provide real banking, financial accounts or financial services. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address or payment card information to play.

Career progress, game records, preferences and purchase-related access state are saved on your device so you can continue playing. Seamen Games does not operate an account server or receive your locally saved career information through the game.

On Android, an optional one-time purchase unlocks the full career. Purchases, payment processing and purchase ownership checks are handled by Google Play. Seamen Games does not receive your payment card details. Google may process transaction and device-related information under its own policies to provide Play billing and related services. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Seamen Banker does not include in-game advertising or developer-operated third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The game does not ask for access to your contacts, camera or location. Android and Google Play may independently provide platform services, diagnostics or purchase records according to their own settings and policies.

If you choose to export or share a game certificate or other game information, you decide where it goes. Any destination app or service you select handles that information under its own privacy terms.

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, subject to Android backup behaviour. Your Google Play purchase record is managed by Google rather than deleted by clearing the game's data.
